TokenMapper: Towards a Universal Interoperability Layer for Speech Tokens
Abstract

Neural audio codecs discretize speech into token sequences, but the resulting token spaces differ in vocabulary and codebook structure, preventing direct communication across models. This limitation affects applications such as conversational voice agents and speech to speech translation systems where multiple speech models must interact. As a result, transferring information between speech systems typically requires decoding to waveform audio and re-encoding with a second tokenizer, increasing latency and introducing potential information loss. To address this limitation, we present TokenMapper, a direction aware framework for direct token to token translation between heterogeneous speech tokenizers in the discrete domain. TokenMapper supports structurally mismatched token spaces, including mappings between single codebook and multi codebook representations. Experiments on GLM-4-Voice, MiMi, and DualCodec show consistent cross model performance. Specifically, translation WER approaches native reconstructions within 2.5-6.8% absolute WER, with UTMOS between 1.95-3.38 and end to end latency reductions of 4.8-94.5%, reaching up to 972 ms per utterance relative to waveform bridging. These results demonstrate practical cross model speech interoperability without intermediate waveform reconstruction.
